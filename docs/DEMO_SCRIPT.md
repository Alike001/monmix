## 0:00–0:25 — promise

"Choose how much MON you want to hold. The desk reads Kuru, values your wallet and explains whether to wait, block or review an adjustment—without moving funds in this release."

Show `/` and the live midpoint card.

## 0:25–0:55 — market truth

Open `/markets`. Point out chain 143, market address, live age, two-sided depth, price precision and the BBO cross-check. Briefly explain that the 10x regression test prevents the researched 0.247 error.

## 0:55–1:55 — portfolio journey

Open `/desk`; connect an injected wallet on Monad. Show real MON and USDC balances. Change target, drift and cap inputs to demonstrate `WAIT`, `BLOCK`, and `REVIEW`. Point out estimated MON, worst visible price and venue refusal states.

## 1:55–2:25 — safety boundary

Emphasize the green review notice: no approval, signature, transaction or contract-enforced cap. Switch to a wrong network briefly to show refusal if practical.

## 2:25–2:55 — evidence

Open `/activity` to show the honest empty state, then `/proof` for pinned contracts, reference SHAs, implemented evidence and the explicit list of missing execution proof.

## 2:55–3:00 — close

"Milestone 1 proves the market-to-portfolio-to-review product. A tiny trade is a separately gated next milestone, not a hidden demo claim."
