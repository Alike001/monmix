# MonMix identity

## Name and promise

- Product: **MonMix**
- Tagline: **Your crypto, your mix.**
- Plain-language promise: MonMix helps people keep MON and USDC aligned with a chosen target, understand portfolio drift, and review protected Kuru trades while retaining wallet control.

The name treats an allocation as a personal mix rather than an automated fund. It is short enough for navigation and makes the two-asset product easy to explain without DeFi terminology.

## Original mark

The mark is an independently drawn, rounded-square monogram. Two strokes form an `M`; the acid-colored inner stroke also reads as two allocations meeting at a shared target. It uses the existing ink, paper and acid palette so the release retains the product's established visual character.

The source-of-truth vector is `apps/web/app/icon.svg`. The header uses the same geometry as inline SVG so it remains crisp and does not add an asset dependency. The mark, social card and copy were created for this project; no Shijima, Masayume, Kuru or Monad brand asset was copied.

## Usage boundaries

- `MonMix` is the public product name; internal workspace package names and legacy browser-storage keys remain stable to protect imports, saved strategy preferences and transaction-journal recovery.
- `safe mode` communicates the public execution-disabled build. It is not a claim that advisory browser limits are enforced onchain.
- Kuru and Monad names identify integrations and network context, not product ownership or endorsement.
