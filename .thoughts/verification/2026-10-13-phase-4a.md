## Deviations From Plan

- No real wallet-extension acceptance was attempted because it would require opening or unlocking a user's Chrome wallet profile. The mock is reported only as a mock.
- With zero allowance, Kuru cannot execute the exact account-bound buy simulation. The product truthfully stops after exact approval simulation; a fresh preparation after a separately confirmed approval is required.
- The watcher is a feasibility design only. No backend, scheduler, notification provider or persistence was created.
- Envio was not added because it does not improve the first owner-confirmed transaction or browser-local recovery path yet.

## Gaps And Risks

- Upgradeable Kuru implementation/code identity can change; the adapter intentionally fails closed.
- Direct calls have no onchain deadline; the client expiry/re-simulation control cannot eliminate wallet latency.
- Exact approval remains usable until consumed or changed; revocation UX is not implemented.
- Browser local storage is not canonical cross-device history and can be cleared or edited.
- Balance-delta evidence may be affected by concurrent wallet activity; Trade logs and receipt status remain the fill authority.
- No funded gas, approval, sell or buy receipt exists for a project-controlled wallet.
- Sponsor-specific current submission terms still require owner review against the official form.
